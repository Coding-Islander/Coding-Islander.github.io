Skills Badges

Complete a Coding Islander workshop and you get a shareable skills badge with its own credential ID. Every badge has a public page anyone can open to confirm it is genuine.

1. Complete a workshop

Finish a hands-on Coding Islander workshop and put the skills into practice.

2. Receive your badge

You get an email with a personal credential link showing your name, the badge and its ID.

3. Share it

Add the badge to your LinkedIn profile, or post it so your network can see what you learned.

Hiring or checking someone's badge? Anyone can confirm a credential in seconds - no account needed.
